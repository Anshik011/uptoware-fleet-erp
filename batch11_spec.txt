## 4. Alert categories

### A. Vehicle & Driver Alerts
- Vehicle insurance expiry
- Fitness certificate expiry
- PUC expiry
- Road tax expiry
- Permit expiry
- Driver licence expiry
- Driver medical fitness expiry
- Vehicle breakdown reported
- Vehicle unavailable for dispatch
- Vehicle maintenance overdue, only if maintenance scheduling data supports it.

### B. Trip Operations Alerts
- Trip dispatched
- Trip awaiting weighbridge confirmation
- Trip awaiting approval
- Trip delivered
- Trip cancelled
- Trip delayed, only where a reliable expected completion time exists.
- Vehicle or driver unavailable for assignment.

### C. Fuel Alerts
- Abnormal fuel consumption
- Fuel efficiency below configured threshold
- Suspicious duplicate fuel entries
- Fuel approval pending
- Fuel log rejected

Use verified fuel benchmarks. Do not create arbitrary thresholds.

### D. Maintenance & Workshop Alerts
- New breakdown reported
- Breakdown awaiting approval
- Vehicle sent to workshop
- Breakdown awaiting spare parts
- Repair completed
- Breakdown open beyond a configurable duration
- Repeated breakdowns, only with a defensible threshold.

### E. Inventory Alerts
- Spare part below reorder level
- Stockout risk
- Material Request pending
- Material Request approved
- Material Issue completed
- Material Receipt completed
- Stock adjustment requiring attention.

Use actual warehouse-level reorder settings and stock balances.

### F. Procurement Alerts
- Purchase Order submitted
- Purchase Order awaiting receipt
- Purchase Receipt completed
- Purchase Invoice pending
- Supplier payment due, where reliable due dates exist.
- Delayed procurement, only when expected dates are configured.

### G. Insurance & Compliance Alerts
Integrate the existing Batch 8 expiry scheduler.

Support:
- Upcoming expiry milestones
- Expired documents
- Missing mandatory documents
- Dispatch blocked due to compliance
- Policy renewal pending
- Renewal completed
- Notification history and duplicate prevention.

Do not create a second expiry-monitoring scheduler.

### H. Revenue & Financial Alerts
- Sales Invoice submitted
- Customer payment received
- Invoice partially paid
- Invoice overdue
- Outstanding receivables exceeding configured thresholds
- Credit note issued
- Operational expense awaiting approval
- Expense approved or rejected.

Do not count Payment Entries as revenue.

Financial notifications must respect accounting permissions and confidentiality.

## 5. Configuration

Extend Fleet Settings where appropriate.

Allow authorized administrators to configure:

- Global notification enable/disable
- Individual alert category enable/disable
- Alert severity
- Recipient roles or authorized users
- Email versus in-app delivery
- Reminder intervals
- Expiry warning windows
- Financial alert thresholds
- Fuel efficiency thresholds
- Breakdown escalation duration
- Inventory alert settings.

Validate all thresholds and intervals.

Do not silently use arbitrary business values.

Use sensible defaults only where already established by previous batches. Clearly document every default.

## 6. Escalation and reminders

Support configurable reminders for unresolved actionable alerts.

Examples:
- Expired statutory documents
- Breakdown awaiting action
- Pending trip approval
- Low-stock spare parts
- Overdue customer invoices.

Requirements:
- No repeated notification spam.
- Respect configured reminder intervals.
- Track previous notifications.
- Escalate only when configured.
- Stop reminders when the underlying condition is resolved.
- Prevent duplicate notifications for the same event and milestone.

Do not create unnecessary background jobs.

Use native Frappe scheduler events and existing scheduled infrastructure.

## 7. Notification history and audit

Provide an accessible notification history with:

- Alert title
- Description
- Category
- Severity
- Source document
- Recipient
- Creation date/time
- Delivery method
- Delivery status
- Read/acknowledged status
- Last reminder time
- Resolution status.

Do not delete historical notification records when an alert is resolved.

## 8. Security and permissions

Define appropriate access for:

- System Manager
- Fleet Manager
- Accountant
- Workshop Supervisor
- Store Manager
- Other authorized users.

Ensure:
- Financial alerts are restricted to authorized finance users.
- Workshop alerts are visible to relevant workshop users.
- Procurement and inventory alerts are visible to authorized store/procurement users.
- Drivers cannot access confidential financial information.
- Users cannot manipulate alert audit history or delivery records.

## 9. Email delivery and local environment

Inspect the existing Email Account and SMTP configuration.

The development environment may not have external SMTP credentials.

Therefore:
- Verify in-app notification creation and delivery.
- Verify email queue creation and configuration.
- Handle missing SMTP configuration safely.
- Do not claim external email delivery is verified without a successful real delivery test.
- Preserve notification records when delivery fails.
- Support later production SMTP configuration without code changes.

Track external SMTP configuration as a production dependency if credentials remain unavailable.
